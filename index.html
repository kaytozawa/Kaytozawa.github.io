About

A life in music.

Classically trained and a graduate of York University’s music program, Kay has been playing violin for 19 years, developing a deep love for classical repertoire and the craft of performance.

On stage, she focuses on clear, honest playing that serves the music first. Off stage, she continues to grow as both a performer and teacher, sharing what she has learned with students and audiences alike.
