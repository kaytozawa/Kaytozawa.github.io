Meet the artist

Meet Kay
Tozawa

Kay Tozawa is a classically trained violinist and a graduate of York University’s music program. She has been playing for 19 years, developing a deep love for the classical repertoire and the craft of performance.

On stage, Kay focuses on clear, honest playing that serves the music first. Off stage, she continues to grow as both a performer and teacher, sharing what she’s learned with students and audiences alike.
